Good uses for torrents on iOS

BitTorrent is a legitimate file distribution protocol. Common legal uses include downloading Linux distribution ISOs, Creative Commons and public-domain films, open-source game assets, academic datasets, and your own files shared between devices.

Tips

  • Only download content you have the right to use.
  • Check file hashes published by the project to verify integrity.
  • Use Wi-Fi for large files and keep enough free storage on your device.
  • Keep iOS and your apps updated for security fixes.
